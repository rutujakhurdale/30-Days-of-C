Day 05 – Logical Operators in C

1. What are Logical Operators?

Logical operators are used to combine two or more conditions or reverse a condition.

2. Types of Logical Operators

AND (&&)

Both conditions must be true.

Example: "1 && 1 = 1"

OR (||)

At least one condition must be true.

Example: "0 || 1 = 1"

NOT (!)

Reverses the result.

Example: "!1 = 0"

3. Truth Table
